### Project: Vienna Advent Calendar

### Overview: 
A single HTML page, called index.html. The CSS and JavaScript should be in folders with those names.  This will be a clickable image of a cartoonish animated map of Vienna. Put the number 1 to 24 in various places around the city, near distinctive places. Numbner 25 should be put on the cathedral in the center. Animate gentle snow falling. 

## Background:
	# Use "background.gif"
	# Overlay the number 1 to 24, spread out across the city in random order.
	# Animate a light snow falling across the scene. 


## Actions:
When a number is clicked, it will start a video file, an image, or a game, which should expand to 95% of the full screen, and have a clickable X for a close button. The assets will be in the "assets" folder, and be numbered corresponding to the date number. For instance, the asset which should be displayed on day 1 (the first of December) will have the prefix 1.

Highlight the number on the map for a specific day of the month. For instance, if it is the 13th of the month, the number 13 should be surrounded by a pulsing light to give it prominence. 

##Functions:
	For development, all of the numbers will be accessible, and display their modal. In production, only the current day and the preceeding days will function. 

To begin, code the above in JavaScript and HTML, so that we can begin to tweak it as necessary.
